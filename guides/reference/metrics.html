Metrics Definitions#

This page defines the primary and secondary outcomes used in the study.

Primary Outcome#

Propagation Rate ($PR_t$)#

Measures the extent to which biased outputs propagate across agents over turns. Reported as an aggregated run-level metric over interaction rounds.

Secondary Outcomes#

Emergence Rate ($ER_t$)#

Captures when bias first appears in the conversation trajectory. Used to profile onset timing across intervention conditions.

Amplification Rate ($AR_t$)#

Measures growth of bias relative to initial biased signals. Used to detect whether interaction dynamics escalate or dampen bias.

System Robustness#

Run-level robustness score derived from final answer behavior and bias metrics. Used as a summary indicator alongside $PR_t$, $ER_t$, and $AR_t$.

Reporting Guidance#

  • Report metrics per intervention condition and protocol.

  • Keep config and data split controls fixed for fair comparisons.

  • Report exclusions and sensitivity analyses when technical failures occur.
